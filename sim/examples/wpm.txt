# Typing-only WPM (CONFIG_NICE_BEETLE_WPM_TYPING_ONLY=y, widgets/wpm_counter.c) - make sim-test
#
# `expect wpm` = the WPM the central screen shows (our counter), `expect wpm_zmk` = ZMK's own
# counter (every key release counts), for comparison.
# Both use ZMK's algorithm: key releases are counted, recomputed every second, and the counters
# restart every 5 s (updates at 1..5 s, 6..10 s, ...; the count is reset after the 5 s, 10 s,
# 15 s ... update). So N counted releases between t = 10 s and 15 s give, at the 15 s update,
#   WPM = N / 5 chars per word / (5 s / 60) = N * 2.4      (25 releases -> 60 WPM)
#
# alphred_kb ALPH-WIN positions: 1..10 = q..p, 12 Tab, 35 Enter, 39 &spund (tap = Space),
# 0 &gresc (Esc), 11 &bckdl (Backspace), 24 LSHIFT, 36 LALT, 37 LGUI, 38 LCTRL,
# 13 &hm LEFT_GUI A, 14 &hm LEFT_ALT S, 40 &mo ARROWS (ARROWS: 8 UP, 19 LEFT, 20 DOWN, 21 RIGHT,
# 6 F12, 10 F5).

at 900 expect wpm == 0

# ---- 10..15 s: 25 letters -> 60 WPM (ZMK agrees)
at 10100 type qwertyuiopqwertyuiopqwert
at 15200 expect wpm == 60
expect wpm_zmk == 60
